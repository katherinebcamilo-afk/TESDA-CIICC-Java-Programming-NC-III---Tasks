About Me

Katherine B. Camilo is a dedicated, creative, and versatile young professional. She was awarded as one of the Most Outsatanding Philippine Society of Information technology Students (PSITS) Presidents in Region 12, CHED Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES) Scholar, Graphics Designer of the Association of Notre Dame Organizations and Social Media Page Moderator. As a former ICT Teacher of Notre Dame of Cotabato, she developed a strong foundation in computer-related areas and specifically taught Responsive Web Designing, Digital Photography and Videography. During her time at Notre Dame of Midsayap College (NDMC), she's one of the major contributors who built a project called “PERA” (Placement and Employment Rate Analytics) a web application that predicts placement rate in SOCCSARGEN Region in her studyn "Data Mining on Applicants' Placement Rate in SOCCSARGEN, Philippines". And her internship at the Office of the Chief of Staff (OACS) for Communications, Electronics and Information Systems (CEIS), H6, AFPHSC in Quezon City allowed her to further hone her experience in software mechanisms.
